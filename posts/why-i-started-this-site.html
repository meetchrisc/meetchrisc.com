Why I started this site

I retired in January, after a long career selling tickets. For years I used the web all day without ever really learning how it worked underneath.

This year that changed. AI tools can now build a website from a plain-language description — you say what you want, and the page appears. I wanted to understand that, not just watch it happen.

So this site is my playground. I'm learning how a page is put together — the words, the layout, the photos — by building one myself and changing it as I go. It will keep changing. That's the point.

I'll write here about what I'm learning, and about the rest of it too: the music, the markets, long walks with Lola, life in Austin.

If you're learning something new in retirement too, you're in good company. More to come.
